Overview

Happy to Be Included is a job-recruitment platform built with Included.ai to help recruiters source and hire qualified, diverse candidates. The project combined stakeholder research with backend engineering to translate real hiring pain points into a working candidate-search product.

The Problem

Recruiters often face a tradeoff between hiring quickly and hiring for diversity, and existing recruitment tools weren't designed to help them do both at once. Working with our sponsor, Included.ai, our team set out to understand where qualified, diverse candidates were being overlooked in the hiring process and to design a platform that made it easier to find them.

Research Process

We conducted a literature review, stakeholder interviews, and surveys with recruiters and hiring managers to understand where existing tools fell short, then used usability testing on our prototypes to validate design decisions before implementation.

My Contribution: Backend & Database
